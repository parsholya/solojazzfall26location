About me

Hi! I'm Olya, and I'm very excited to share my experience and passion for solo jazz dancing with you. I've been dancing and teaching for 15 years and would love to welcome you to our community class.

After years of ballet, folk, and modern dance, I fell in love with swing and have been dancing, teaching, and competing since 2011. I teach Lindy Hop and solo jazz with Vermont Swings in Burlington, and my favorite part is helping people discover just how much fun swing dancing can be. That's why I decided to bring it even closer to home, to Middlebury.

Whether it's your first class or your fiftieth, I hope you'll come join us!
